## Moodbow Privacy Policy
Last updated: 6 November 2026

Moodbow is a private journal made by Vitor Ribeiro Carvalho, trading as BurrowSoft (297 Moo 1, Baan Sanian, Mueang Nan, Nan 55000, Thailand), "we" in this policy. We wrote this to be read, not skimmed past. If anything is unclear, email support@moodbow.com.

### The short version
{pending:bo_off}- Only you can read your journal. BurrowSoft staff don't read journals; direct database access is limited to maintenance by Vitor Ribeiro Carvalho. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your journal to train AI.{/pending}
{pending:bo_live}- Your journal is private to your account. BurrowSoft staff don't read journals; direct database access is limited to maintenance by Vitor Ribeiro Carvalho. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your journal to train AI.
- Bo, our optional AI, is available in selected countries. If you use it, your messages go to our AI provider, Anthropic, in the USA. If you turn on its journal switch, so do your recent check-ins and entries.{/pending}
{pending:ai_live}- AI features are optional. If you turn them on, the parts of your journal needed for a reflection or report are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, in the USA.{/pending}
- You can export everything, or delete your account and everything in it, at any time, from the app{pending:web_journal_live} or the website{/pending}.

### 1. What we collect
**Your account:** your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash), and when you agreed to our Terms and this policy, and confirmed you're 18 or older.
**When you last connected:** the time your account last connected to our servers. We use it to keep you signed in and, for accounts without an email, to apply the 12-month rule below.
{pending:anon_start_live}**Using Moodbow without an email:** you can start without giving us an email. We create an account for your phone that only it can use, and store your journal there like any other account. If you add an email later, your journal stays the same account. If you never add an email and don't open the app while connected to the internet for 12 months, we delete that account and its journal.{/pending}
**Your journal:** the entries you write, your daily check-ins (mood, sleep, movement (active minutes), stress), the dates they belong to, and which entries you mark as moments.
**Your settings:** language, country, time zone, reminder time, and your AI choices ({pending:ai_live|bo_off}on or off, tone, focus areas{/pending}{pending:bo_live}Use Bo and its journal switch{/pending}).
{pending:ai_live|bo_off}**AI content (only if AI is on):** the reflections and reports the AI writes for you{pending:ai_memory}, and the short facts Moodbow remembers about you, which you can see, edit and delete in Me → What Moodbow remembers{/pending}.{/pending}
{pending:ai_live|bo_off}**AI usage:** for each AI request, the feature, the model, the number of tokens, the cost and the credits used. Not the text of your entries or of the AI's answer.{/pending}
{pending:purchases}**Purchases:** if you buy AI credits, Google Play or the App Store handles the payment. We receive a purchase reference and the number of credits, never your card details.{/pending}
{pending:bo_live}**Your conversation with Bo:** your messages and Bo's replies, stored in your account until you clear them or delete your account. A conversation can hold up to 20,000 messages; past that, Bo tells you nothing more is being saved.{/pending}
{pending:bo_live}**Crisis checks:** if you use Bo, we check your messages and Bo's replies for signs you may be in crisis, on your device and on our servers, so Bo can point you to help. We don't keep a record of these checks, and nobody at Moodbow is told. Your conversation itself is stored in your account, so it may show that Bo offered help, until you clear it or delete your account. Bo can't contact anyone for you.{/pending}
{pending:bo_live}**Reports:** if you report a reply from Bo, we keep a copy of that reply and any note you add. Vitor Ribeiro Carvalho reads reports. We delete them after 90 days, unless we need one longer to deal with illegal material.{/pending}
{pending:bo_live}**Your country, for Bo:** to decide whether Bo is available where you are, our website reads your country from your connection and gives the app a short-lived signed answer. We don't store your IP address or country for this, though our hosting provider's logs record connections, as described under Technical logs.{/pending}
{pending:bo_live}**Bo's limits:** we record that a request to Bo happened and when, so we can apply daily limits. This doesn't include your text.{/pending}
**Error reports:** if the app or website crashes or another error happens, a technical report (device type, app or browser version, the error) is sent to our error-tracking provider. We remove personal details and any text you've written before it's sent, and the provider doesn't store your IP address.

**Cookies and storage on your device:** the website uses only the cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in, and one that remembers whether you view your Timeline as a list or a calendar. It also keeps a few display choices in your browser, such as light or dark appearance and the cards you've already closed. None of them track you across sites or are used for advertising or analytics.

**What stays only on your device:** unsent drafts{pending:app_app_lock}, your app-lock PIN{/pending}{pending:app_share_cards}, and the share cards you create. Share cards are made on your device and only leave it if you share them yourself{/pending}.{pending:bo_live} What you've typed to Bo but not yet sent stays on your device too.{/pending}{pending:age_signal_live} So does the result of your app store's age check, if it gives one. We don't receive your birthdate.{/pending}

**Technical logs:** like almost every online service, our hosting providers record IP addresses and device or browser information when you connect, for security and to fix problems. They keep these logs for a limited time (see §4). We also keep daily totals of AI use ({pending:bo_off}how many reflections were made that day{/pending}{pending:bo_live}how many AI requests were made that day{/pending} and what they cost us), with no link to any person, so we can keep costs under control.
**Emails you send us:** if you write to support@moodbow.com, we keep the conversation so we can answer you, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

We don't collect your name, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and we don't use advertising trackers.

{pending:web_analytics}**Visit counts (public website only):** we count visits to our public pages with a cookie-free tool from our host, Vercel: which page was opened, from what kind of device and country, and which of our campaign links brought you there, if any. It uses no cookies or identifiers, never runs in the web journal, never sees what you write, and can't tell who you are. The mobile app sends no analytics.{/pending}

### 4. How long we keep it
- Everything is kept until you delete it or delete your account, except as this policy says.
- **Deleting your account** removes your journal, check-ins, {pending:bo_live}your conversation with Bo and your reports, {/pending}{pending:ai_live|bo_off}AI content, {/pending}settings, {pending:ai_live|bo_off}credits history {/pending}and login from our live database immediately.
{pending:bo_live}- Clearing your conversation with Bo deletes it from our live database right away. If you delete your account, we also ask Anthropic to delete the ID we gave it, but Anthropic may keep its safety records.{/pending}
- Copies in our encrypted backups disappear automatically within {pending:backup_retention}8{/pending} days.
- Error and crash reports are kept by our error-tracking provider for up to 90 days.
- Technical logs at our providers (hosting, database, email delivery) are kept only for a limited period set by each provider, and then deleted.
- When you delete your account, copies in those providers' logs also expire under these retention periods.

### 5. Your rights
Wherever you live, you can:
- **see and download** everything: Me → Export my data;
- **correct** it: edit any entry, check-in or setting;
- **delete** it: delete any item, or your whole account in Me → Delete account;
{pending:ai_live}- **withdraw your AI consent** at any time in Me → AI. This stops new AI requests; past reflections stay until you delete them;{/pending}
{pending:bo_live}- **withdraw your consent to Bo** at any time in Me. This stops new messages; your past conversation stays until you clear it. Export includes your conversation and your reports;{/pending}
- **object or ask questions**: email support@moodbow.com. We answer within 30 days.
You can also complain to a data-protection authority: in Thailand, the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC); in the EU or UK, your local authority.

### 6. Security
Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Database rules make sure each account can only ever read its own rows. {pending:app_lock}You can add an app lock (PIN or fingerprint/face) on your phone.{/pending} No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever affects your data, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

### 7. Age
Moodbow is for people aged 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we'll delete it.{pending:bo_live} Bo is also only for people aged 18 and older. We may stop access to Bo if we learn someone is under 18.{/pending}{pending:age_signal_live} If your phone's app store tells us you're under 18, Moodbow{pending:bo_live} and Bo{/pending} won't open, but you can still export or delete your journal and reach the helplines.{/pending}
