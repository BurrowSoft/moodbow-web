---
title: The PDF journal
summary: Save a month or a year of your journal as a PDF.
topic: Privacy & account
---
{pending:plus}With Moodbow Plus, **Me → Designed PDF of your journal** lays out a month or a year of your journal as a PDF: a cover, then each day's check-in and entries, with each month on a new page.

Choose **A month** or **A year**, then **Save as PDF or print**. Your browser's print window opens, where you can choose "Save as PDF" or print it.

In the app, choose **Share as PDF** to save or send it.

The PDF is made on this device and isn't saved anywhere by Moodbow. Anyone with the file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

On the web without Plus, you can still print a month: open it in **Timeline** and choose **Print this month**.{/pending}
