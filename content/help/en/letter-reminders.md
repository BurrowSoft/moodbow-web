{pending:app_letters|letters_live}When you write a letter to future me, choose its reminders under **Reminders**:
- **On the day:** on to start with, at 9:00. You can change the time or turn it off.
- **Add a reminder:** up to 3 heads-ups before the day, **1 day**, **1 week**, **2 weeks** or **1 month before**, each with its own time and switch. Tap a heads-up's label to change how long before. Days that would fall on or before today aren't offered.

Reminders arrive in the Moodbow app on your phone, also for letters you write on the website, which sends nothing itself. They only say that a letter from you opens soon, never what you wrote, and they need notifications on for Moodbow on your phone.

Reminders follow their own switches, not your daily check-in reminder. Opening a letter early cancels its reminders, and deleting it removes them.

{pending:plus}Letters are part of **Moodbow Plus**. Reminders for letters you've already written keep coming, Plus or not.{/pending}{/pending}
