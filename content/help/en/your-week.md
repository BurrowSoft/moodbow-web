{pending:weekly_report_live}In **Insights**, the **Your week** card looks back at your last complete week (Monday to Sunday). Tap **See my week** and Moodbow writes four short parts: what changed, what went well, something worth noticing, and one small idea.

It needs check-ins on at least 3 days that week, and AI turned on in **Me → AI**. Each week has one report; delete it if you'd like a new one. Moodbow only reads that week's check-ins and entries, and the week before for comparison.

Like reflections, Your week isn't advice or a diagnosis. If something you wrote that week suggests you're struggling, Moodbow won't write a report; it shows **Want to talk to someone?** instead.{/pending}
