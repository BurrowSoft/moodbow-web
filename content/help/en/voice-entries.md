{pending:app_voice}In the app, you can speak your entry instead of typing it. Tap the microphone in the corner of the writing space, on **Today** or while you write an entry, and talk. Your words appear as text as you speak. Tap the microphone again to stop: while it listens, it says **Listening… tap to stop**. Then change the text if you like, and save it as usual.

The first time, Moodbow explains what happens, then your phone asks whether Moodbow can use the microphone. If you said no, you can allow it later in your phone's settings.

**Your voice stays on your phone.** Your phone turns your speech into text on the phone itself. Your voice never leaves your phone, and no recording is kept: only the text you keep is saved, like any entry. Listening stops when you leave the app.

**No microphone?** Moodbow shows it only on phones that can turn speech into text on the phone itself, in the language you use in Moodbow. On Android, that needs Android 12 or later. If something goes wrong, for example the language isn't downloaded on your phone, Moodbow says **Voice isn't available on this phone right now.**

Voice entries are for everyone{pending:plus|app_plus}, Plus or not{/pending}. They're only in the app: on the web, a browser's speech feature would send your voice to its maker's servers, so Moodbow doesn't offer it there.{/pending}
