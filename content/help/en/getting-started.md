Moodbow is a private journal for your story. Each day you can log how you feel in a few seconds, and write about your day when you feel like it. Over time, **Timeline** and **Insights** show how your sleep, movement and stress line up with your mood.

**Your first minute**
- In the app, write a few words about your day, or tap **Skip for now**. Signing up is optional: your journal is saved to an account for this phone.{pending:web_journal_live} On the web, sign in with an email.{/pending}
- When you're ready, add an email in **Me → Back up your journal**, so you can sign in on another phone{pending:web_journal_live} or on the web{/pending}.
{pending:ai_live}- Pick what you'd like Moodbow to pay attention to, and how it should talk to you.{/pending}
{pending:app_reminders}- Choose a daily reminder time on your phone, or turn reminders off.{/pending}
- Do your first check-in on **Today**.

{pending:web_journal_live}Once your journal has an email, you can use Moodbow in the app and on the web at moodbow.com with the same account. Everything stays in sync.{/pending}

Tap the tab you're on to jump back to its top.

There are no streaks and nothing to keep up with. Missing days is normal. Moodbow is here when you are.
