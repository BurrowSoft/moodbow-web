Moodbow is a private journal that learns your story. Each day you can log how you feel in a few seconds, and write about your day when you feel like it. Over time, your timeline and charts show what makes good days better.

**Your first minute**
- Create your account and confirm your email.
{pending:ai_live}- Pick what you'd like Moodbow to pay attention to, and how it should talk to you.{/pending}
- Choose a daily reminder time on your phone, or turn reminders off.
- Do your first check-in on **Today**.

You can use Moodbow in the app and on the web at moodbow.com with the same account. Everything stays in sync.

Tap the tab you're on to jump back to its top.

There are no streaks and nothing to keep up with. Missing days is normal. Moodbow is here when you are.
