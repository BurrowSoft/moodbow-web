title: Want to talk to someone?
summary: Where to find support if you're struggling.
topic: Help
---
If you're in danger right now, call your local emergency number.

{pending:help_now_live}If you're signed in, **Me → Want to talk to someone?** shows the numbers for your country. In the app it's also on the lock screen, and on the web at the top of **Print this month**. If we don't have numbers for your country yet, it links to a directory where you can find one.{/pending}

{pending:app_today_help}The lifebuoy at the top of **Today** opens the same help, whatever else is on the page. Its label is **Want to talk to someone?**{/pending}

Moodbow is a journal, not a medical service, and it isn't a substitute for professional help.
