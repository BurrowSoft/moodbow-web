{pending:share_cards}Your month's moods can become a card shaped like the Moodbow rainbow, one band per day. Open it from **Insights** or a month in **Timeline**, preview it, then share it.

The card is made on your device and only leaves it if you share it. By default it shows only colours and counts, never what you wrote.{/pending}

{pending:share_cards_more}**More cards:** in the share sheet, switch the card type to **Chart** for the shape of your mood over the last 7 days, 30 days or 3 months (no numbers or dates), or **Milestone** when you reach 30, 100 or 365 days journaled.{/pending}{pending:year_card} In December and January there's also **Year**: twelve bands, one for each month.{/pending}
