---
title: Moments
summary: Star the entries you want to find again.
topic: Your journal
---
Mark an entry as a **moment** (★) when it's a day you want to remember. Moments show a star wherever the entry appears. Tap ★ at the top of an entry to make it a moment, and again to unmark it.

{pending:timeline_moments_rpc}In **Timeline**, switch to **Moments** to see only your starred entries.{/pending}
