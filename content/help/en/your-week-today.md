---
title: Your week on Today
summary: What the Your week card on Today means.
topic: Your journal
---
{pending:your_week_live}On **Today**, **Your week** looks back at your last complete week, Monday to Sunday. It's worked out from your check-ins with fixed rules, with no AI, and it never reads what you write. It shows once you've checked in with a mood on at least 4 days that week.

It says one or two short things:
- **A pattern,** when one stands out: "On days you slept more, your mood was higher.", "On more active days, your mood was higher." or "On lower-stress days, your mood was higher." Under it: "This may show what tends to go together, not what causes what."
- **How the week looked:** "Your week was mostly steady.", "Your week had more good days than low ones." or "Your week had good days and low ones."
- **A moment from that week,** if you starred one, with its date. Tap it to open the entry.

If most of your days were low, or half of them were and none were good, the card says only **"This looked like a hard week."**, with **Want to talk to someone?**{pending:safety_plan_live}, and **My safety plan** if your plan has something in it{/pending}. Nothing about it is recorded.

Your week is for everyone, Plus or not. It isn't advice or a diagnosis.{/pending}
