{pending:showed_up_live|app_showed_up}**Days you showed up** is how many days you checked in. It's a count, nothing more: there's nothing to keep up with, and Moodbow never sends you a reminder about it. Missing days is normal.

**Where you'll see it:**
- On **Insights**, under the tabs, for the time you're looking at: for example, **You showed up on 12 days in the last 30 days.** **Week** is the last 7 days, **Month** the last 30 days and **3 months** the last 3 months.{pending:plus} Insights are part of **Moodbow Plus**, which shows it for each range, including **Year** (the last 12 months) and **All time** (since you started).{/pending}
- On the **My month's moodbow** card you can share{pending:plus} (Moodbow Plus){/pending}, as **Showed up on 12 days**.
{pending:year_review_live}- In **Your year in Moodbow**, for the whole year: for everyone, from 1 December.{/pending}

**What counts:** each day you checked in, including past days you add later. A day with only an entry and no check-in doesn't count. If you didn't check in during that time, there's no line at all.

It's worked out from your check-ins, with no AI, and nothing new is stored.{/pending}
