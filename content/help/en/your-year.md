{pending:year_review_live}**Your year in Moodbow** looks back at your year in a few short screens. From 1 December to 31 January it's on **Today**, and in January it shows the year that just ended. You can always open it from **Insights**.{pending:plus} It's for everyone, Plus or not.{/pending}

It appears once you've checked in on at least 30 days that year.

**What it shows:**
- **Your year in colours:** each month, the moods you checked in with.
- **Days you showed up:** how many days you checked in.
- **Your moments:** how many moments you marked (★), and the dates of your first, a middle one and your last.
- **Things that help you:** a few of your own things that help, in your order, and the tags you often used on good days.
- **A pattern,** when one stands out, such as "On days you slept more, your mood was higher." It may show what tends to go together, not what causes what.

If low moods filled much of your year, the moments, things that help and pattern screens are replaced by one line, **"This year asked a lot of you. You kept showing up."**, with **Want to talk to someone?**.

It's worked out from your check-ins with fixed rules, with no AI, and nothing new is stored. It never reads what you write.

**Share your year** makes an image with your year's colours, the number of days you showed up and the year. Your entries, tags, moments and dates are never on it; you can add a line of your own if you like. It's shared only if you choose to, through your phone's or browser's own share options.{/pending}
