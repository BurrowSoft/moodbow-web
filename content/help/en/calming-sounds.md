title: Calming sounds
summary: Rain, waves, brown noise or a gentle hum, with a timer.
topic: Your journal
---
{pending:sounds_live|app_sounds}In **Me → Things that help me**, tap **Calming sounds**. Choose **Rain**, **Waves**, **Brown noise** or **Gentle hum**, pick a **Timer** and tap **Play**. You can **Pause**, **Stop** or switch to another sound at any time.

Moodbow makes the sounds as they play, on your phone or in your browser, so there's nothing to download. Use your device's volume.

**Timer:** choose how long it plays, or **Until I stop**. At the end, the sound fades out. A sound left paused for 30 minutes stops by itself.

{pending:app_sounds}**In the app:** it keeps playing with the screen off or while you use another app, with **Pause** and **Stop** in its notification. On the lock screen it says only **Sound playing** (and the minutes left), never which sound. A call or another app's sound pauses it; tap **Play** to carry on.{/pending}

{pending:sounds_live}**On the web:** it keeps playing when you switch tabs, and your device's media controls say only **Sound playing**. Leaving the page or signing out stops it.{/pending}

If your phone or browser can't make the sounds, Moodbow says so. Moodbow doesn't record which sound you play or for how long. **Want to talk to someone?** is on this screen too.

Calming sounds are for everyone{pending:plus|app_plus}, Plus or not{/pending}.{/pending}
