{pending:app_appointment|appointment_live}In **Me → For my appointment**, make a summary to print or show at an appointment. Under **Show**, choose **Last 2 weeks**, **Last 4 weeks** or **Last 12 weeks**.

The summary shows:
- how many check-ins you made in that time;
- **Averages** for mood, sleep, stress and movement, with small charts once there are a few days to show;
- your **Moments**: each one's date and first line. Tick **Include the full text** to add a whole entry.

In **What I want to talk about**, note what you'd like to bring up.

It uses only your check-ins and Moments: never your letters, your safety plan or your list of things that help. Nothing here is saved or sent: when you leave, your choices and notes are gone.

{pending:appointment_live}On the web, tap **Print**. To keep a copy, choose to save as PDF in your browser's print window.{/pending}{pending:app_appointment}In the app, tap **Share as PDF** to send or save it.{/pending}

The summary is made by you from your own entries. It isn't a medical assessment or advice.{/pending}
