---
title: Writing entries and drafts
summary: Write about your day; your words are kept as you go.
topic: Your journal
---
Tap **Write about your day** to start an entry. Write as much or as little as you like (up to 20,000 characters), and change the date if it's about an earlier day.

**Drafts:** while you write, Moodbow keeps a draft on your device. If you leave before saving, Moodbow keeps the draft for next time, or asks if you'd rather discard it.

**Edited on another device?** If the same entry changed somewhere else while you were editing, Moodbow asks which version to keep: yours or the other one. Nothing is overwritten without asking.

You can delete an entry from the entry itself. Deleting can't be undone.
