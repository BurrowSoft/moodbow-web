{pending:plus}With Moodbow Plus, **Insights → Year in review** shows a year at a glance: how many days you checked in each month, your mood month by month, your sleep, movement and stress over the year, how many entries and moments you wrote, and your moments by date.

It's made only from what you logged and wrote, with no AI. It doesn't score your year or pick a best or worst month.

Choose another year from the list at the top. A month with fewer than 5 check-ins is left out of the mood chart.{/pending}
