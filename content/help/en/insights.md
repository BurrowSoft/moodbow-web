---
title: Insights and charts
summary: See how your mood, sleep, stress and exercise change over time.
topic: Your journal
---
**Insights** shows charts for your mood, sleep, stress and exercise over the last week, month or 3 months, with your average and how it compares with the period before.

Charts appear once you have a few check-ins in that period. The comparisons are just information, never a score: there's no "good" or "bad" direction.
