title: Your picture in Me
summary: Put a picture of your choice at the top of Me.
topic: Settings
---
{pending:me_picture_live|app_me_picture}At the top of **Me**, tap the big card (the rainbow, with the pencil) to put a picture of your choice there. Choose a picture, then crop it to the card. It's for everyone.

The picture isn't part of your journal, so it's never sent to us and isn't in your export. {pending:me_picture_live}On the website it's kept only in this browser. {/pending}{pending:app_me_picture}In the app it's kept on your phone, and in your phone's own backup if you use one. {/pending}Location and other hidden details are removed when you choose it. Another browser or device shows the rainbow until you choose a picture there too.

**Remove picture** brings the rainbow back. Signing out or deleting your account removes it too.{/pending}
