{pending:trusted_share_live|app_trusted_share}In **Me → Share your week**, you can make a private link for someone you trust, like your therapist or a friend. It's read-only, and they don't need an account to open it.

**What they see:** a week at a time: the mood colour of each day you checked in, and how many check-ins you made. If you choose, also how often you ticked your **Routine** items, as a percentage of your check-in days. They never see what you write, your tags, moments, sleep, movement or stress.

**From today on:** a link never shows anything from before the day you make it. They see the current week and can go back up to 3 weeks, but never past that day.

**Make a link:** tap **New link**. If you like, add their first name: their page greets them with it. Choose what to **Include from your routine**, and when the link ends: after **7 days**, **30 days** (the default) or **90 days**. **What they'll see** shows exactly what they'll get. By creating the link, you agree to share this summary with whoever has it. If you use **App lock**, Moodbow asks you to unlock first.

**Copy it now:** after **Create link**, tap **Copy link** and send it yourself. For your privacy, Moodbow can't show it again. If you lose it, stop it and make a new one.

**Your links:** **Me → Share your week** lists each link, with when it ends and how many times it's been opened. You can have up to 3 active links, and **Me** shows how many are active. **Today** tells you when a link is made and when it's first opened, so you'd know if it wasn't you.

**Stop sharing:** tap **Stop sharing** and confirm: the link stops working at once. Ended and stopped links stay in your list for 30 days, then they're deleted. Deleting your account ends them all.

Anyone who has the link can see the summary until it ends or you stop it, so send it only to the person it's for. Their page isn't indexed by search engines.{/pending}
