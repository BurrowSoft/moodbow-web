{pending:app_routine|routine_live}**Routine** is a short list of things you'd like to tick off each day: your medication, a walk, a therapy exercise. It's optional. Only you see it, unless you choose to put it in your appointment summary{pending:trusted_share_live} or in a link you share{/pending}.

**Set it up:** in **Me → Routine**, type a name, such as **Morning meds**, and tap **Add**. You can have up to 3, each up to 40 characters. The menu beside an item has **Edit**, **Move up** and **Move down**.

**Tick it off:** when you check in, tap each item under **Routine** that you did that day, and tap it again to untick it. If the ticks can't be saved, for example without a connection, Moodbow says **Your routine ticks weren't saved. Try again.** Your check-in itself is still saved.

**Patterns:** on **Insights**, **Patterns in your check-ins** can show a line for an item: "On days you ticked Morning meds, your mood averaged 3.8 of 5. On other days, 3.1." It shows only when your mood was at least half a point higher on the days you ticked it, never when it was lower or about the same. Like the other patterns, it uses the last 3 months and needs at least 14 check-ins, with at least 5 ticked days and 5 other days. Days before you added the item don't count. Under it: "This may show what tends to go together, not what causes what. Never change a medication without talking to your doctor or pharmacist."{pending:plus} Insights are part of **Moodbow Plus**.{/pending}

**For my appointment:** turn on **Show in For my appointment** for an item, and the summary shows how often you ticked it: "Morning meds: ticked on 80% of check-in days". With fewer than 5 check-in days to go on, it says **not enough days yet**.

**Remove:** choose **Remove** in the item's menu and confirm. This removes the item and the days you ticked it.

Your routine is included when you export your data. Deleting a day's check-in removes that day's ticks. Moodbow's AI never reads your routine, and item names are never counted. If a name sounds like you might need support, Moodbow offers help, as it does with tags.{/pending}
