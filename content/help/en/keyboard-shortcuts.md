{pending:shortcuts_live}On the web, press **?** to see the shortcuts: **n** writes a new entry, **c** opens today's check-in, **t** goes to Timeline, **i** to Insights, and **/** opens search on Timeline. They don't work while you're typing in a field, so they never get in your way.

They follow the letters your keyboard types, so with a Thai keyboard layout switch to English to use them. If you use voice control or they get in the way, turn them off in **Me → Appearance → Keyboard shortcuts**.{/pending}
