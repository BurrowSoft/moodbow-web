---
title: Daily reminders
summary: A gentle nudge to check in, at the time you choose.
topic: Settings
---
In **Me → Daily reminder** (in the app or on the web), turn the reminder on or off and choose its time. Your phone sends the reminder itself, so it works even without a connection, and it never shows anything you wrote. If you've already checked in that day, there's no reminder.

The reminder itself arrives on your phone, from the Moodbow app. If notifications are off for Moodbow in your phone's settings, turn them on there.
