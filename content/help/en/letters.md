{pending:app_letters|letters_live}In **Me → Letters to future me**, write to yourself and choose when to read it. Under **Open it**, choose **In 1 month**, **In 3 months**, **In 6 months** or **In a year**, then tap **Save letter**. Once it's saved, a letter can't be edited.

{pending:plus}Writing a new letter is part of **Moodbow Plus**. The letters you've already written are always yours, Plus or not: they open on their day, and you can open them early, delete them and export them at any time.{/pending}

Until its day, a letter is locked: **Timeline** shows **A letter from you · opens on** its date, never what you wrote. Locked means Moodbow keeps it out of sight until then; it's stored as privately as the rest of your journal.

On the day, Today shows **A letter from you, written on** the day you wrote it. If more than one is due, the one you wrote first comes first. Tap **Open** to read it, or **Not now**: that letter comes back in a week, and Today shows no letter card again that day on this device. If **Want to talk to someone?** was showing above it, that stays on Today for the rest of the day.{pending:app_letters} In the app, if your daily reminder is on, you also get a notification that day. It never shows the letter.{/pending}

**Open early:** open a letter from **Me → Letters to future me** before its day, and choose **Open now** or **Keep it for later**.

**Delete:** tap **Delete** and confirm. The letter is gone for good.

If a letter mentions something that sounds like you might need support, Moodbow shows help when you write it and when you open it.

Letters aren't part of search, **Insights** or the appointment summary, and Moodbow's AI never reads them. They're included when you export your data, locked ones too.{/pending}
