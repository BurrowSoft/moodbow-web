topic: Your journal

{pending:moment_recall_live}**On this day** on **Timeline** brings back your moments (★), never ordinary entries, 1 month, 6 months or a year after their day.

Under a moment's text, the **Bring this back in** card lets you choose when: **1 month**, **6 months** and **1 year**, in any combination. All three are on to start with, and a chip that's on shows ✓. Turn all three off and the entry stays a moment but never comes back as a memory.

Your choice is saved straight away. It's kept with the entry, included when you export your data, and deleted with the entry. **Hide for today** on a memory works as before, and entries about very hard times are never brought back.

Bring this back in is free.{/pending}
