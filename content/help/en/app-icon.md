---
title: App icon
summary: Choose a different Moodbow icon for your home screen.
topic: Settings
---
{pending:app_icons}In the Moodbow app, **Me → App icon** changes the icon on your home screen: the Moodbow rainbow, **Night**, **Sunrise**, **Five moods**, **Calm sea** or **Simple**. Your phone may show a short note when the icon changes.

{pending:plus}Choosing an icon is part of **Moodbow Plus**. If Plus ends, the icon you chose stays; only choosing a new one needs Plus.{/pending}

A website has no home-screen icon to change, so App icon is in the app only.{/pending}
