summary: Check in with one tap from your home screen.
topic: Settings
---
{pending:widget_live}For now, the widget is on Android only. Add the **Moodbow** widget from your phone's widget picker. Tap a face to open Moodbow with that mood already picked; nothing is saved until you tap **Save**. After you check in, the widget shows **Checked in** with today's face.

The widget never shows anything you wrote. If **App lock** is on, the faces still show before you check in; after you check in, it shows only **Checked in**, with no face or mood word. Opening Moodbow asks you to unlock it first.

The widget uses the language you chose in Moodbow.{/pending}
