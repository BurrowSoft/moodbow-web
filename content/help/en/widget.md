{pending:widget_live}Add the **Moodbow** widget from your phone's widget picker. Tap a face to open Moodbow with that mood already picked; nothing is saved until you tap **Save**. After you check in, the widget shows **Checked in** with today's face.

The widget never shows anything you wrote. If **App lock** is on, it shows no face and no mood word, and opening Moodbow asks for your PIN first.{/pending}
