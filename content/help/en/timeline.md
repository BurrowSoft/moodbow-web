**Timeline** shows your days, newest first, with your mood and a preview of each entry. Open a day to see its check-in and everything else from it. Days you didn't log simply don't appear; there are no gaps to feel bad about.

Tap a day to see everything from it, edit it, or add a check-in or an entry for that day.
