{pending:app_safety_plan|safety_plan_live}If you're in danger right now, call your local emergency number.

In **Me → My safety plan**, write down what can help you through a hard time, so it's there when you need it. Fill it in on a calmer day. Every part is optional.

It has six parts:
1. **Warning signs:** what tells you a hard time might be starting.
2. **Things that help me:** the same list as **Me → Things that help me**, so a change in one place shows in both.
3. **People and places that take my mind off things**
4. **People I can ask for help:** tap **Add a person**, then a **Name** and, if you like, a **Phone number (optional)**. With a number, **Call** opens your phone's dialler.
5. **Professionals and services:** **My doctor or therapist**, added the same way, and the **Helpline** for the country in **Me**. Moodbow always shows it, and you can't remove it.
6. **Making my surroundings safer**

Tap **Save** under a part when you've written in it. Each of parts 4 and 5 holds up to 10 people.

Once your plan has something in it, **My safety plan** also appears under the numbers in **Want to talk to someone?**{pending:weekly_report_live}, and beside it on a hard **Your week**{/pending}. An empty plan shows no button.

**Who can see it:** only you. Moodbow's AI never reads your plan. It's included when you export your data, and deleted with your account.{pending:app_safety_plan}

In the app, your plan also opens without a connection, as it was when it last loaded. With **App lock** on, the plan stays behind the lock. To reach it without unlocking, turn on **Show my safety plan without unlocking** in **Me → App lock**: you confirm with your PIN, fingerprint or face first. Then the lock screen shows **My safety plan**, to read and to **Call**. Anyone holding your phone could read it, so it's off unless you turn it on.{/pending}

If things feel too much, call someone or tap **Want to talk to someone?**{/pending}
