---
title: Deleting your account
summary: How to delete your account and everything in it.
topic: Privacy & account
---
Go to **Me → Delete account**, type DELETE, and confirm. Your entries, check-ins, AI reflections and reports, what Moodbow remembers, your settings and your sign-in details are deleted right away. This can't be undone{pending:ai_live}, and any AI credits are lost{/pending}.

Consider **exporting your data** first. Encrypted backups are removed automatically within 8 days.

Can't sign in? Email support@moodbow.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.
