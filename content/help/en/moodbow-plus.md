---
title: Moodbow Plus
summary: What's free, what Plus adds, the free week, and how to cancel.
topic: Privacy & account
---
{pending:plus}Your journal is free. Insights come with Plus. Help and your data are free for everyone, always.

**Free:** check-ins and your journal with no limit, the Timeline, the calendar and search, help and your safety plan, things that help you and grounding, the appointment summary, export, import and delete.

**Plus adds:** Insights (week, month, 3 months, your year and all time), patterns and What lifts me, Year in review, letters to future me, more themes (and app icons in the app), a designed PDF of your journal, a prompt library to write from, more ways to breathe in **Things that help me**, and more reminders. The prompt library's Hard days prompts are free for everyone.

**More patterns** can include your own tags, once you use them.

{pending:lifts_live}**What lifts me** compares your mood on days with a tag to days without it, once a tag has been used on at least 5 days. It shows patterns, not causes, and it never shows some tags, such as ones about alcohol or skipping meals.{/pending}

**The free week:** Plus starts with 7 days free, once per person. After the 7 days it becomes a yearly subscription and renews each year until you cancel. You only start it yourself, from **Me → Moodbow Plus**. During the free week, Me shows how many days are left.

**Cancel:** you can cancel at any time, and you keep Plus until the end of the time you've paid for. If you cancel during the free week, you won't be charged.
- Bought on the web: **Me → Moodbow Plus → Manage Plus**.
- If you have Plus through Google Play or the App Store, cancel it there, under your subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.

**If Plus ends:** everything you wrote stays, and every free feature keeps working. The Plus views lock again, and your theme goes back to the default. Nothing is deleted.

**Deleting your account** cancels Plus bought on the web straight away. If you have Plus through Google Play or the App Store, cancel it there too: deleting your account doesn't stop the store's subscription.{/pending}
