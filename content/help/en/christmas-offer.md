{pending:offer_live}From 1 to 31 December, Moodbow Plus has a Christmas special offer: a lower price for your first year. You'll see it in **Me** and on the Plus screen, with the price in your currency.

**Who it's for:** anyone who has never had Moodbow Plus, or its free week, on the website or any store. While the offer runs, it takes the place of the 7-day free week.

**After the first year,** Plus renews automatically at its usual yearly price, which is shown before you buy.

**Cancelling:** you can cancel at any time. Bought on the website: **Me → Moodbow Plus → Manage Plus**. Bought in the app: in Google Play or the App Store, under your subscriptions.

The offer ends at the start of 1 January (UTC). It never shows next to help, your safety plan, or while you're writing.{/pending}
