{pending:pictures_live|app_pictures}When you star an entry as a moment (★), you can add up to 3 pictures to it. Open the entry and tap the camera tile. You choose them with your phone's or browser's own photo picker, so Moodbow never asks for your photo library.

{pending:plus}Adding pictures is part of **Moodbow Plus**. The pictures you've already added are always yours, Plus or not: you can see them, export them and delete them at any time.{/pending}

Before a picture is uploaded, Moodbow makes it smaller and removes its hidden details, such as where it was taken. It's stored with your journal, visible only to you. Moodbow's AI never sees your pictures, and they're never in share cards or notifications.

Pictures show in the entry, as small thumbnails on **Timeline**, and as a 📷 on that day in the calendar.

**Limits:** up to 3 pictures on a moment, and a monthly amount for each account. When you reach it, Moodbow says so, and you can add more next month.

**Deleting:** tap × on a picture. Deleting the entry or your account deletes its pictures too. If you remove the star, Moodbow asks whether to keep the pictures or delete them. When you delete a picture, its entry or your account, it disappears from Moodbow straight away; for up to a minute a copy you'd opened may still be reachable from your own account, and no one else can reach it. Deleted files are removed from our live storage within a day.

**Export:** **Me → Your data → Export my data** includes your pictures: a zip with your data file and a folder of pictures. If a picture can't be fetched, the export still finishes and says the pictures are missing.{/pending}
