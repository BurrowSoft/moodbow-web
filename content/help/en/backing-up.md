---
title: Backing up your journal
summary: Add an email so your journal is safe and you can use it on another phone.
topic: Privacy & account
---
You can start Moodbow in the app without an email. Your journal is saved to an account that belongs to this phone. Adding an email backs it up: if you lose or change your phone, you can sign in and everything is still there.

**How to add an email**
1. In the app, open **Me → Back up your journal** (or tap **Add an email** on the card on **Today**).
2. Enter your email and tap **Send the link**.
3. Open the email from Moodbow and tap the button to confirm. On your phone, **Open Moodbow** takes you back to the app.
4. Choose a password. That's it: same journal, nothing moves.

{pending:web_journal_live}After that you can also sign in on the web at moodbow.com.{/pending}

**If the email already has a Moodbow account:** you can use a different email, or sign in to that account instead. The journal on this phone can't move to it: **Sign in instead** deletes the journal on this phone for good (Moodbow asks you first). Tap **Export first** if you want to keep a copy.

**Journals without an email:** if you never add an email and don't open the app while connected to the internet for 12 months, we delete that journal. Moodbow reminds you a month before.

Without an email there's no **Sign out**, because you couldn't sign back in. **Delete account** works as usual.
