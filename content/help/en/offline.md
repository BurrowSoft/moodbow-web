---
title: Using Moodbow offline
summary: What happens when you have no connection.
topic: Your journal
---
You can check in and write while offline. Check-ins you save offline are kept on your device{pending:web_journal_live} (in the app) or in your browser (on the web){/pending} and sync automatically when you're back online. An entry you write offline is kept as a draft: save it again once you're back online.

{pending:app_offline_start}In the app, if you've signed in before, Moodbow also opens without a connection and shows the journal saved on your phone.{/pending}

If the same day's check-in was changed on another device in the meantime, Moodbow shows you both versions and asks which one to keep. If something saved offline can't be synced, you'll see a short note so you can add it again.
