{pending:ai_live}On a saved entry, tap **Reflect with Moodbow** for a short, kind reflection on what you wrote and one gentle question to think about. Tap **Write your answer** to add the question to your entry and answer it in your own words. Each entry has one reflection; delete it if you'd like a new one.

**Turning AI on or off:** AI is optional and needs your consent. Turn it on or off in **Me → AI**. To write a reflection, Moodbow sends that entry and your recent check-in numbers to our AI provider, Anthropic. They aren't used to train AI models.

**Moodbow AI Lite:** when the full Moodbow AI isn't available, Moodbow keeps going with **Moodbow AI Lite**, a simpler model with shorter answers and a daily allowance.

Moodbow's AI isn't a therapist and doesn't give medical advice. If you're struggling, use **Want to talk to someone?**.{/pending}
