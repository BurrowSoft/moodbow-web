summary: Check in straight from your daily reminder.

{pending:app_quick_checkin}In the app, tap your daily reminder and Moodbow opens with **How are you today?** over Today (after **App lock**, if it's on). Tap a face and your check-in is saved straight away.

**Saved** and **Undo** stay for 5 seconds, or, with a screen reader on, until you use or close them. **Undo** puts the day back as it was. If you close it sooner, **Undo** stays on Today until the 5 seconds are up.

To add sleep, movement, stress or an entry, open the check-in on Today and tap **Edit**, as usual. Choose the reminder's time in **Me → Daily reminder**.

The one-tap check-in is only in the app, not on the web.{/pending}
