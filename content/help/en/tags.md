{pending:tags_live}Tags are a few words of your own on a day's check-in: who you saw, what you did, how it felt. They're part of your journal and free for everyone.

**Add tags:** when you check in, type a tag under **Tags** and press Enter, or pick one from **Your tags**. You can add up to 5 tags a day, each up to 32 characters. Tap the × on a tag to remove it.

**See them:** your tags show on the day's check-in and in the **Timeline**. Search finds them too.

**Export and import:** tags are in your export: a **tags** column in the spreadsheet, and on the day's line in the readable file. When you import from Daylio, its activities come in as tags.

**Delete:** removing a day's check-in removes its tags. Deleting your account deletes them with everything else.

{pending:plus}**Patterns:** with Moodbow Plus, **More patterns** in **Insights** can show how your mood goes on days with your most-used tags.{/pending}

If something you write in a tag sounds like you might need support, Moodbow offers help, just as it does when you write an entry.{/pending}
