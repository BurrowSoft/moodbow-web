title: Signing in and passwords
summary: Confirmation links, forgotten passwords, and signing out.
topic: Privacy & account
---
**Confirming your email:** after you sign up, we send a link to your inbox. Open it and tap **Confirm email**. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or ask for it again.

**Forgot your password?** Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a link to choose a new one. After you change it, you're signed out on all your devices.

Confirmation and password-reset links work for 24 hours. If yours has expired, ask for a new one in the app{pending:web_journal_live} or on the web{/pending}.

{pending:web_journal_live}On the web, if something you wrote isn't saved yet when you sign out, Moodbow asks whether to keep it on that browser or delete it.{/pending}
