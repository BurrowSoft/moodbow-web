---
title: App lock
summary: Keep your journal behind a PIN or your fingerprint or face.
topic: Settings
---
{pending:app_app_lock}In the app, open **Me → App lock** to choose a 4–6 digit PIN, and turn on fingerprint or face unlock if your phone has it. You can also choose when it locks: immediately, after 1 minute or after 5 minutes.

Your PIN stays on your phone and is never sent to us. While the app is locked, its preview in your phone's app switcher is covered{pending:widget_live}, and the home-screen widget shows nothing personal{/pending}.

**Forgot your PIN?** Tap **Forgot PIN?** to sign out. When you sign in again, the lock is cleared and you can set a new one. App lock is only in the app, not on the web.{/pending}
