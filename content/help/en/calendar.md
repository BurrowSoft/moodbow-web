{pending:calendar_live}On **Timeline**, switch from **List** to **Calendar** to see one month at a time. Each day you checked in has a dot in that day's mood colour; a ★ marks a moment, and a small line means you wrote an entry. Days without a check-in just show their date: missing days is normal.

Use ‹ and › to move between months, and tap a day to open it. **Share this month** makes the same moodbow card as in Insights. Moodbow remembers whether you prefer the list or the calendar on this device.{/pending}
